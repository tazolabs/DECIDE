01 / Overview

What should a voice agent do next?

A caller says “hello?” Is it a greeting, a request to repeat, or an interruption? The words alone may leave the decision unresolved. Voice agents need to combine what was said with how it sounded and what happened earlier in the call.

DECIDE evaluates these local decisions using short clips from real production conversations between agents and callers. Each question pairs an audio clip with permitted context and a set of candidate decisions. The task is to choose the decision supported at the end of the clip.

The benchmark covers 570 source calls and 34 agents, predominantly in college admissions, with English and Hindi speech. A clip can support multiple questions. Reference decisions were manually annotated by a team of four.
