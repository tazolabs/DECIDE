01 / Overview

Evaluating decisions in real conversations.

DECIDE tests a model’s ability to make decisions during real voice-agent calls. Each question combines a short audio clip with relevant conversation history and a set of candidate answers, covering business intent, graph routing, tool use, turn taking, emotion and language.

05 / FAQ

DECIDE benchmark FAQ

What does DECIDE measure?

DECIDE measures whether a model can make the right decision at a specific moment in a voice-agent call, using a short audio clip, permitted context and candidate options.

Its six decision families cover business and conversation, graph routing, tool decisions, turn taking, voice properties and language switching.

What data is the benchmark built from?

DECIDE v1.0 contains 4,867 decision questions across 1,081 clips from real production voice-agent conversations. Clips are 3–25 seconds long and support 32 decision tasks.

A clip can support multiple questions. Each question asks for a decision supported by the conversation up to that clip’s cutoff.

Can I compare audio and text-based models?

Yes. Direct-audio models receive the clip and its permitted context. STT + text models receive a transcription and context instead.

Both choose from the question’s candidate decisions. Audio can carry cues such as tone, interference and overlapping speech that a transcript may not preserve.

How are models scored?

Accuracy measures how often a model’s selected decision matches the human reference label. Failed or missing predictions count as incorrect.

Hosted evaluations return aggregate scores by task, decision family and clip duration. Results for groups with fewer than 10 questions are withheld.

How can I evaluate my model?

Submit a compatible model API endpoint on the evaluation page. The service checks it on a public example, runs the private benchmark and returns aggregate results. No invitation is needed.

Start with the GitHub evaluation instructions to review the API contract and test your integration.

Is the full benchmark publicly available?

The full benchmark remains private. You can listen to six examples here or explore 20 labelled samples on Hugging Face.

Hosted evaluation does not return dataset files or reference labels. Your model API operator receives the audio or text and context sent for inference.
